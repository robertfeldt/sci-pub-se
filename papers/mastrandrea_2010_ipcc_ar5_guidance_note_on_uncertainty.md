# Mastrandrea et al. (2010), "Guidance Note for Lead Authors of the IPCC Fifth Assessment Report on Consistent Treatment of Uncertainties"

**Links:** https://www.ipcc.ch/site/assets/uploads/2017/08/AR5_Uncertainty_Guidance_Note.pdf

IPCC Cross-Working Group Meeting, Jasper Ridge, July 2010. 6 pages: the *operational* document authors actually follow.

- **Two metrics** for communicating certainty in key findings:
  - **Confidence** (qualitative): very low / low / medium / high / very high.
    - Based on **evidence** (type, amount, quality, consistency: *limited / medium / robust*) and **agreement** (*low / medium / high*).
    - Shown as a 3×3 evidence × agreement matrix, with confidence increasing toward the top-right.
  - **Likelihood** (quantified probability), used when evidence allows. Calibrated terms:
    - *virtually certain* 99–100%
    - *very likely* 90–100%
    - *likely* 66–100%
    - *about as likely as not* 33–66%
    - *unlikely* 0–33%
    - *very unlikely* 0–10%
    - *exceptionally unlikely* 0–1%
  - Calibrated terms are *italicised* in the reports.
- **Traceable account:** every key finding needs a chapter-text description of how the evidence and agreement were evaluated.
- **Process advice:**
  - Plan early how certainty will be communicated.
  - Agree in advance on a balanced process for collective judgements.
  - Be aware of Type I vs Type II error framing.
  - Report **tails** of distributions (low-probability, high-consequence outcomes).
  - Make findings general enough to be supported, but not vacuous.
- Responds to the **2010 InterAcademy Council review** of the IPCC, after the AR4 errors controversy.

**Teaching hooks**
- The closest analogue to GRADE outside medicine. It grades *findings in a synthesis*, not individual papers, just as GRADE rates a *body of evidence*.
- SE thought experiment: rewrite the key findings of an SE survey or SLR with IPCC-calibrated language. "It is *likely* (medium confidence) that TDD increases…"
- "Traceable account" ≈ what reviewers should demand from SE discussion sections.
