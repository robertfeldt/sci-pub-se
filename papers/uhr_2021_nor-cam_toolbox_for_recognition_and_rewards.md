# Universities Norway (UHR) (2021), "NOR-CAM – A toolbox for recognition and rewards in academic careers"

**Links:** https://www.uhr.no/en/_f/p3/i86e9ec84-3b3d-48ce-8167-bbae0f507ce8/nor-cam-a-tool-box-for-assessment-and-rewards.pdf

Working-group report, Oslo, May 2021, CC-BY. A further development of the EU's OS-CAM (2017).

- **Aim:** assess a *wider* range of academic work, recognising quality, scope and **degree of openness**, for appointments, promotions and project applications.
- **Six principles:**
  1. Measure quality by balancing qualitative and quantitative measures.
  2. **"Everybody should not do everything"** (differentiated profiles).
  3. Open science is a fundamental principle.
  4. Transparency: state what earns merit.
  5. Gender balance and diversity.
  6. Local design of announcements and processes.
- **Bibliometrics critique (3 points):**
  - never use bibliometrics in isolation;
  - they **look backward**, while hiring must look forward;
  - they **ignore context** (career stage, resources, collaboration types).
- **The matrix has 6 categories:**
  - research output (incl. data, code, videos);
  - research process;
  - service and leadership;
  - research impact;
  - teaching and supervision;
  - professional experience.
- **"From set menu to à la carte":** not every "dish" is needed for every "meal". Each vacancy announcement should state which categories it weights.
- **Practical recommendations:**
  - Applicants select **3–5 works** for *qualitative* assessment, together with a **reflection** on their significance and use. The basis for assessment is documented results plus reflection.
  - The "Achilles' heel" is documentation. The report proposes a national **"automagic CV"** system (Cristin's CV is too limited).
- **International context:** DORA, Leiden, Science Europe, EUA, the Hong Kong Principles, and the Dutch "Room for everyone's talent".

**Teaching hooks**
- Exercise: write the 3–5 work selection plus a 100-word reflection for your own CV. How does that differ from listing papers?
- Swedish participants: is there an equivalent (SUHF, VR)? Mostly not at this level, which makes a good contrast.
- Link to SE: software and data count as research output, which matters for tool-building SE researchers.
