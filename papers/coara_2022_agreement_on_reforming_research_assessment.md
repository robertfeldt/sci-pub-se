# CoARA (2022), "Agreement on Reforming Research Assessment" (20 July 2022)

**Links:** https://coara.org · PDF: https://www.coara.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/2022_07_19_rra_agreement_final.pdf-3.pdf

Drafted via Science Europe, EUA and the European Commission; Coalition for Advancing Research Assessment. 700+ signatories, including many Norwegian and Swedish universities and funders.

- **Vision:** assessment should recognise *diverse* outputs, practices and activities. It should be based **primarily on qualitative judgement, with peer review central**, *supported* by responsible use of indicators.
- **Scope:** assessment of organisations/units, projects, and individual researchers/teams. *Not* country-level performance.
- **Principles:**
  - Integrity and ethics first.
  - Protect freedom of research.
  - Respect organisational autonomy.
  - Independent, transparent data and infrastructure, with the community owning critical tools.
  - Focus on quality and impact, including openness and reuse.
  - Recognise diversity of roles, careers and missions.
  - Gender equality, equal opportunities and inclusiveness.
- **Four core commitments:**
  1. Recognise the diversity of contributions and careers: teaching, leadership, data, software, open science, outreach.
  2. Base assessment primarily on qualitative evaluation (peer review), supported by responsible metrics.
  3. **Abandon inappropriate uses of journal- and publication-based metrics.** Named explicitly: **JIF, AIS and h-index** as quality proxies. "Inappropriate" includes relying solely on author metrics (counting papers or citations) and judging outputs by venue, format or language.
  4. **Avoid using university rankings** in research assessment, so that commercial ranking methods don't trickle down to individual researchers.
- **Six supporting commitments:**
  - (5) Commit resources to the reform.
  - (6) Review and develop criteria, tools and processes.
  - (7) Raise awareness and train assessors.
  - (8) Exchange practices.
  - (9) Report progress.
  - (10) Evaluate the effect, keeping the reform evidence-informed.
- **Timeline:** an action plan within **1 year** of signing (or by end 2023); full review cycle within **5 years** (touchpoint end 2027).
- **Not anti-metrics:** indicators remain allowed as *support*. The target is *inappropriate* use. Builds on DORA (2012) and the Leiden Manifesto (2015).

**Teaching hooks**
- "Your university probably signed this. Does your hiring or promotion committee act like it?"
- Norway: removed publication points from funding (2025) and has NOR-CAM. Sweden: increases bibliometric weight in the funding model from 2026, while its HEIs sign CoARA. Is that a contradiction?
- What does commitment 3 mean for a PhD student choosing between ICSE and a "Q1 journal"?
