# IJzerman, Lewis, Przybylski, Weinstein, DeBruine, Ritchie, Vazire, Forscher, Morey, Ivory & Anvari (2020), "Use caution when applying behavioural science to policy"

**Links:** https://doi.org/10.1038/s41562-020-00990-w · accepted version: https://orca.cardiff.ac.uk/id/eprint/135538/

Nature Human Behaviour 4:1092–1094. File: Cardiff ORCA accepted version (16 pp.). **Figure 2 (PDF p. 13) shows the ERL ladder as an image; Figure 1 (p. 12) shows NASA's TRLs.**

- **Context:** COVID-19. Behavioural scientists rushed to advise policy (Van Bavel et al. 2020) on evidence that wasn't ready. Problems include publication bias, WEIRD samples, unvalidated measures and lack of generalisation tests.
- **Proposal:** Evidence Readiness Levels, a behavioural-science variant of NASA's TRLs (1–9). The authors call it "a very preliminary step" that doesn't map perfectly onto TRLs.
- **The 9 levels:**
  1. Define the problem *with the stakeholders* likely to implement the intervention.
  2. Develop the concepts with people in the target settings (how might context change things?).
  3. Systematic reviews and meta-syntheses with bias detection. Even then, findings reach "at most ERL3".
  4. Stimulus and measurement validity and equivalence in the target setting.
  5. Weigh benefits and harms with local experts.
  6. Estimate effects in a pilot sample.
  7. Test heterogeneity in low-stakes samples and settings.
  8. Test heterogeneity in higher-stakes samples and settings.
  9. Apply in the real target setting or crisis, with evaluation continuing and feedback loops.
- **Communicate the level to policy-makers:** are we making "educated guesses (ERL3 or below)", or have we tested and replicated in representative environments (ERL7)?
- **Climbing is incremental:** "An ERL6 study built on a shaky ERL1 foundation will be of little use." Moving up requires investment and **epistemic humility**.

**Teaching hooks**
- Striking for SE: a well-done systematic review is only ERL3. Most SE "evidence for practitioners" sits there, or is a single study at ERL1–2.
- "What ERL is your contribution?" Benchmark results vs field use. SE people know TRLs from industry projects.
- Complements GRADE/CERQual (how certain is the finding?) with a different question: is it ready to *use*, here? Same spirit as Cartwright.
